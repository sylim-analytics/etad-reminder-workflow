⏱️ eTAD Reminder Workflow

AI-assisted reminders with human review · employee vs. contractor two-track routing

How the workflow evolved

Process discovery

Observed how admins in two directorates handled reminders; synthesized one systematic process with defined targets and standing exceptions.

Sept 2026 · Copilot agent

Built a reminder agent that worked well and was shared with a peer admin for input.

Oct 2026 · Cowork

Advanced it in Cowork to generate individualized Outlook drafts and pull in response/status updates.

Stakeholder-informed

With an engineering-services manager and a peer admin, moved contractor handling to supervisors.

Current two-track logic

Input — monthly missing-hours report
AI prep — read report, verify directory, apply exceptions, build drafts
Route — employee vs. contractor
Human review — approve before send
Update — track responses/status

Key design decision: employees receive an individual reminder with their supervisor copied; contractors are not emailed directly — their supervisor receives a summary of the contractor's missing hours to review and handle. Every draft is reviewed by a human before anything sends.

Missing-Hours Report (fictional)

The recurring trigger: a monthly report of who still owes eTAD hours. Each person is flagged as Employee or Contractor and mapped to a supervisor.

AI-Assisted Processing & Draft Preparation

Reproduces what the Copilot/Cowork step does: read the report, verify each person against the directory, apply standing exceptions, then build individualized Outlook drafts — all staged for review, never auto-sent.

Two-Track Routing

👤 Employee path

  • Individual reminder to the employee
  • Supervisor copied (CC)
  • Lists the employee's own missing hours

🧰 Contractor path

  • No direct email to the contractor
  • Summary sent to the supervisor only
  • Supervisor reviews and handles the hours
